Crypt Cards: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Crypt Cards is an offline game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your best scores, unlocked heroes and powers, and any levels you create in the level editor are saved in the app's local storage on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app is.
