## Where it appears

The icon of the **Scythe** power. It sits on a small button in the top bar during play, and on the locked reward card of the room that unlocks it. Unlocked by clearing the Barracks. In play: Cut down every monster, trap and poison in the 8 cards around you. It is shown small, so one bold shape beats detail.

## What it must look like

- A reaper's scythe (cuts down everything around you). Today it reads more like a pickaxe: make the curved blade much clearer, with a dark handle (`#3e2731`) and a glinting blade (`#c0cbdc`).

## Do not

- Do not use colours outside the palette.
- Do not make it look like the Scythe weapon (`scythe`) or the Tremor icon.
