The icon of the **Mass healing** power. It sits on a small button in the top bar during play, and on the locked reward card of the room that unlocks it. Unlocked by clearing the Sewers. In play: Heal 2, and make every potion on the board 2 stronger. It is shown small, so one bold shape beats detail.
