## Where it appears

A **Scythe** card. It appears in: Barracks, Armory. In play: Step toward a monster to sweep: the target and the two squares beside it (across your swing) all take the scythe's power. The scythe wears down by the most damage it deals to one target. Once picked up, it is also drawn small next to the hero.

## What it must look like

- A farm-style scythe: long brown handle (`#c28a54` / `#733e39`) and a curved grey blade at the top (`#c0cbdc` / `#8b9bb4`).
- It sweeps three squares, so the blade should look wide.

## Do not

- Do not add a background or a frame: only the icon, on transparency.
- Do not make it look like the Scythe power icon (`p_scythe`).

## Deliver

`scythe.png` (at least 256 × 256, transparent; see `README.md`). Optionally up to three variants as `scythe-1.png`, `scythe-2.png`, `scythe-3.png`.
