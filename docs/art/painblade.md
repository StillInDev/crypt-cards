## Where it appears

A **Blade of pain** card. It appears in: Barracks, Armory. In play: While it lies on the board it gains 1 power every time you take damage. Once you pick it up it stops growing and fights like a normal sword. Once picked up, it is also drawn small next to the hero.

## What it must look like

- The Sword's shape, with a dark purple-red blade (`#68386c` / `#a22633`) and a drop or two of red (`#e43b44`) falling from it.
- It grows stronger every time you are hurt.

## Do not

- Do not use colours outside the palette.
- Do not add gore beyond a drop or two.
