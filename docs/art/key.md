# Request: Key

- **Sprite id:** `key` (deliver `key.png`)
- **Kind:** interface icon
- **Read first:** `README.md` (size, palette and style rules)
- **References:** `art/key@8x.png` (current version, to improve on), `art/tomb@8x.png` (object style)

## Where it appears

The fallback reward icon: shown on the reward card and on the map when a room rewards something that is not a hero or a power.

## What it must look like

- A gold key (`#fee761` / `#feae34`), ring at the top, teeth at the bottom, standing upright.
- Shown as the reward icon when a room's reward is not a hero or a power.

## Do not

- Do not use colours outside the palette.

## Deliver

`key.png` (18×18) and `key@8x.png`. Optionally up to three variants as `key-1.png`, `key-2.png`, `key-3.png`.
