# Request: Key

- **Sprite id:** `key` (deliver `key.png`)
- **Kind:** interface icon
- **Read first:** `README.md` (format and style rules)
- **References:** `src/img/sprites/key.png` (current version), `src/img/sprites/tomb.png` (object style)

## Where it appears

The fallback reward icon: shown on the reward card and on the map when a room rewards something that is not a hero or a power.

## What it must look like

- A gold key (`#fee761` / `#feae34`), ring at the top, teeth at the bottom, standing upright.
- Shown as the reward icon when a room's reward is not a hero or a power.

## Do not

- Do not add a background or a frame: only the icon, on transparency.

## Deliver

`key.png` (at least 256 × 256, transparent; see `README.md`). Optionally up to three variants as `key-1.png`, `key-2.png`, `key-3.png`.
