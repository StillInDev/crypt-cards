## Where it appears

A **Fire wand** card. It appears in: Bone Library, Ember Halls. In play: A wand with 6 charges. Step toward an adjacent monster to fire a beam from you to the far edge of that row or column. It burns every monster on the way for its power: fire monsters are immune, ice monsters take double. Once picked up, it is also drawn small next to the hero.
