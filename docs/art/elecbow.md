## Where it appears

A **Electric bow** card. It appears in: Bone Library, Armory. In play: A bow with 5 arrows. Tap any monster on the board to shoot it for the bow's power. The shock then jumps to every monster touching the one it hit, 1 weaker with each jump. Frozen monsters are not hit. Stepping into a monster with a bow is just like having no weapon. Once picked up, it is also drawn small next to the hero.

## What it must look like

- A wooden bow (`#c28a54` / `#733e39`) with an arrow nocked, crackling yellow sparks (`#fee761`) along the string.
- The shock chains between monsters, so the electric feel is the point.
