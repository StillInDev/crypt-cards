# Request: Teleport orb (power)

- **Sprite id:** `p_teleport` (deliver `p_teleport.png`)
- **Kind:** power icon
- **Read first:** `README.md` (format and style rules)
- **References:** `src/img/sprites/p_teleport.png` (current version), `src/img/sprites/tomb.png` (object style)

## Where it appears

The icon of the **Teleport orb** power. It sits on a small button in the top bar during play, and on the locked reward card of the room that unlocks it. Unlocked by clearing the Bone Library. In play: Tap any card. You jump there without using it, and it vanishes. It is shown small, so one bold shape beats detail.

## What it must look like

- The **Teleport orb**: a glowing blue orb (`#0099db` / `#124e89`) with a white highlight and a faint ring or swirl around it.

## Do not

- Do not add a background or a frame: only the icon, on transparency.

## Deliver

`p_teleport.png` (at least 256 × 256, transparent; see `README.md`). Optionally up to three variants as `p_teleport-1.png`, `p_teleport-2.png`, `p_teleport-3.png`.
