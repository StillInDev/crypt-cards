## Where it appears

A **Rat** card. It appears in: Cellar (also its map icon), Sewers. In play: Deals damage equal to its number. If it stays put for 3 turns, it bites a neighbouring card (even another rat) for its number; an empty square, or a card it destroys, becomes another rat just as strong. When rats bite the same card, only the biggest bite lands. The number on the card is its health.

## What it must look like

- Currently a grey rat face. Draw the **whole rat** side-on or three-quarter: grey body (`#8b9bb4` / `#5a6988`), pink ears, nose and tail (`#ff8fa8`), small red eye.
- It breeds (turns neighbours into rats), so a scruffy, twitchy look fits. It is also the Cellar's map icon.
