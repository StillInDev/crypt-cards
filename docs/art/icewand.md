## Where it appears

A **Ice wand** card. It appears in: Frozen Cavern, Bone Library. In play: A wand with 6 charges. Step toward an adjacent monster to fire a beam from you to the far edge of that row or column. Every monster it hits but does not kill freezes. Ice monsters are immune, fire monsters take double. It can kill a Phoenix. Once picked up, it is also drawn small next to the hero.
