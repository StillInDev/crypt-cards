# Request: Healing (power)

- **Sprite id:** `p_heal` (deliver `p_heal.png`)
- **Kind:** power icon
- **Read first:** `README.md` (format and style rules)
- **References:** `src/img/sprites/p_heal.png` (current version), `src/img/sprites/tomb.png` (object style)

## Where it appears

The icon of the **Healing** power. It sits on a small button in the top bar during play, and on the locked reward card of the room that unlocks it. Available from the start. In play: Restore 5 health. It is shown small, so one bold shape beats detail.

## What it must look like

- A big red heart (`#e43b44` / `#a22633`) with a white shine.

## Do not

- Do not add a background or a frame: only the icon, on transparency.
- Do not make it green (that is Mass healing).

## Deliver

`p_heal.png` (at least 256 × 256, transparent; see `README.md`). Optionally up to three variants as `p_heal-1.png`, `p_heal-2.png`, `p_heal-3.png`.
