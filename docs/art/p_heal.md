## Where it appears

The icon of the **Healing** power. It sits on a small button in the top bar during play, and on the locked reward card of the room that unlocks it. Available from the start. In play: Restore 5 health. It is shown small, so one bold shape beats detail.

## What it must look like

- A big red heart (`#e43b44` / `#a22633`) with a white shine.

## Do not

- Do not use colours outside the palette.
- Do not make it green (that is Mass healing).
