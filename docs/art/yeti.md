## Where it appears

A playable hero, shown in game as **Yeti**. It stands on the board as the hero card and is shown large on the home screen. Unlocked by clearing the Frozen Cavern. In play: Thick fur: monsters hit you for 1 less.

## What it must look like

- Currently a white furry head with a blue face. Redraw as a **full figure**: a big shaggy white beast (`#ffffff` lit, `#c0cbdc` shade) with a blue face (`#0099db`) and small dark eyes.
- Broad, rounded body and thick arms; fur edges can be a little ragged. Its power is thick fur (monsters hit it for 1 less), so it should look padded and tough.
- Friendly, not a monster. A small smile is fine.

## Do not

- Do not use colours outside the palette.
- Do not draw claws dripping blood or an open roaring mouth.
