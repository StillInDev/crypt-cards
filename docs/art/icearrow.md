## Where it appears

A **Ice arrow** card. It appears in: Frozen Cavern, Bone Library. In play: The arrow turns every turn. Tap it to fire: the whole row or column it points along slides one square that way and every monster in it takes its number as ice damage and freezes if it survives. You only slide if you are in that line.

## What it must look like

- Same arrow as the Fire arrow, pointing straight up, but the head is ice (`#2ce8f5` / `#0099db`).
- The game rotates it, so it must point exactly up and be centred.

## Do not

- Do not use colours outside the palette.
- Do not draw it diagonally.
