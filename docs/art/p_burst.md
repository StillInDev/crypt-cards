## Where it appears

The icon of the **Potion burst** power. It sits on a small button in the top bar during play, and on the locked reward card of the room that unlocks it. Unlocked by clearing the Fungus Caves. In play: Tap a potion. It explodes and hits neighbouring monsters for its number. It is shown small, so one bold shape beats detail.

## What it must look like

- The **Potion burst**: a four-pointed yellow burst (`#fee761` / `#feae34`). Ideally show a small red potion bursting in the middle so it links to potions.

## Do not

- Do not use colours outside the palette.
