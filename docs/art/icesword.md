## Where it appears

A **Ice sword** card. It appears in: Frozen Cavern, Ember Halls, Armory. In play: An ice weapon. It cannot hurt ice monsters and hits fire monsters for double. A monster it hits but does not kill freezes solid, and a frozen monster cannot be hit: you swap places with it. It can kill a Phoenix. Once picked up, it is also drawn small next to the hero.

## What it must look like

- The Sword's shape, but the blade is ice (`#2ce8f5` / `#0099db`) with a white edge.
