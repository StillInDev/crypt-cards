A playable hero, shown in game as **Ninja**. It stands on the board as the hero card and is shown large on the home screen. Unlocked by clearing the Armory. In play: Quick fingers: +1 gold from every coin and gem. Silent feet: traps cannot hurt you.

## What it must look like

- Shown in game as the **Ninja**. Currently a dark hood with a red mask band, head only. Redraw as a **full figure** in a dark slate outfit (`#3a4466` / `#262b44`), with a red headband or sash (`#e43b44`).
- Only the eyes show through the mask. A light, crouched or ready stance: quick fingers and silent feet.
- Optional: a ninja star at the belt (match `ninjastar`).

## Do not

- Do not use colours outside the palette.
- Do not make the outfit pure black: it must still read on the darkest card colours.
