## Where it appears

A **Viper** card. It appears in: Sewers. In play: Deals damage equal to its number, and its bite poisons you for that many turns too: 1 damage a turn, never below 1 health. The number on the card is its health.

## What it must look like

- The **Viper**. A coiled green snake (`#63c74d` / `#3e8948`), head raised, a forked red tongue (`#e43b44`).
- Its bite poisons you, so one or two drops of venom from the fangs are welcome (poison green `#63c74d`).
