# Request: Fire arrow

- **Sprite id:** `firearrow` (deliver `firearrow.png`)
- **Kind:** trap (object)
- **Read first:** `README.md` (size, palette and style rules)

## Where it appears

A **Fire arrow** card. It appears in: Bone Library. In play: The arrow turns every turn. Tap it to fire: the whole row or column it points along slides one square that way and every monster in it takes its number as fire damage. You only slide if you are in that line.

## What it must look like

- An arrow pointing **straight up**, orange-red shaft and head (`#f77622` / `#a22633`) with flame on the head.
- The game rotates the sprite to show which way it fires, so it must point exactly up and be centred.

## Do not

- Do not use colours outside the palette.
- Do not draw it diagonally.

`firearrow.png` (18×18) and `firearrow@8x.png`. Optionally up to three variants as `firearrow-1.png`, `firearrow-2.png`, `firearrow-3.png`.
