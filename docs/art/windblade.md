## Where it appears

A **Wind blade** card. It appears in: Barracks, Armory. In play: While it lies on the board it gains 1 power every time it slides to a new square. Once you pick it up it stops growing and fights like a normal sword. Once picked up, it is also drawn small next to the hero.

## What it must look like

- The Sword's shape, with a pale cyan blade (`#2ce8f5` / `#c0cbdc`) and two or three short wind streaks trailing behind it.
- It grows stronger as it slides around the board, so the streaks should say "speed".
