## Where it appears

A **Hive queen** card. It appears in: Fungus Caves, Throne Room. In play: Every 4 turns it turns a neighbouring card into a bat. Deals damage equal to its number. The number on the card is its health.

## What it must look like

- The **Hive queen**: an orange and yellow bee queen with a small gold crown (`#fee761`), striped body (`#feae34` / `#3e2731`), wings out.
- It spawns bats every 4 turns, so it should look like a queen: bigger and grander than a normal insect.
