## Where it appears

A **Sword** card. It appears in: Barracks, Armory. In play: Pick it up to arm yourself. Step toward a monster to hit it for the sword's damage. If it dies, you take its place. Each hit wears it down by the health it cuts through. Once picked up, it is also drawn small next to the hero.

## What it must look like

- A plain steel sword, diagonal from bottom-left (hilt) to top-right (tip): grey blade (`#c0cbdc` / `#8b9bb4`), brown grip, gold crossguard (`#feae34`).
- Also drawn in the hero's hand, so keep it slim. All other swords build on this one.

## Do not

- Do not add a background or a frame: only the icon, on transparency.

`sword.png` (at least 256 × 256, transparent; see `art/README.md`). Optionally up to three variants as `sword-1.png`, `sword-2.png`, `sword-3.png`.
