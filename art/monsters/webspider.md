## Where it appears

A **Web spider** card. It appears in: Fungus Caves. In play: It hides in its web while you hold any weapon: its health drops to 0 and you can walk straight through it. Bare-handed, it is a real monster. The number on the card is its health.

## What it must look like

- A dark slate spider (`#3a4466` / `#262b44`) with red eyes.
- While the hero holds a weapon it hides in its web (the card then shows `web`), so it should look sneaky.

## Do not

- Do not add a background or a frame: only the icon, on transparency.
- Do not make it purple or blue (those are the Cave and Ice spiders).

`webspider.png` (at least 256 × 256, transparent; see `art/README.md`). Optionally up to three variants as `webspider-1.png`, `webspider-2.png`, `webspider-3.png`.
