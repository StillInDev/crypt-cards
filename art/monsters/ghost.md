A **Ghost** card. It appears in: Catacombs, Bone Library. In play: Deals damage equal to its number. The number on the card is its health.

## What it must look like

- A white sheet ghost (`#ffffff` / `#c0cbdc`) with a wavy bottom edge and two dark eyes.
- It floats: no floor strip. Simple and classic.
- It must be told apart from the Wraith, Banshee and Frost wisp at a glance (today all four share one shape). Keep the plain sheet look here.

## Do not

- Do not add a background or a frame: only the icon, on transparency.
