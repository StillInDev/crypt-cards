## Where it appears

The icon of the **Poison dart** power. It sits on a small button in the top bar during play, and on the locked reward card of the room that unlocks it. Available from the start. In play: Flies at a random monster. It takes 4 damage and dies if that is enough. It is shown small, so one bold shape beats detail.

## What it must look like

- The **Poison dart**: a small dart, diagonal, steel tip and green poison drop (`#63c74d`), red fletching (`#e43b44`).
