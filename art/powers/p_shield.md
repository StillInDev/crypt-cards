# Request: Shield (power)

- **Sprite id:** `p_shield` (deliver `p_shield.png`)
- **Kind:** power icon
- **Read first:** `art/README.md` (format and style rules)
- **References:** `src/img/sprites/powers/p_shield.png` (current version), `src/img/sprites/monsters/tomb.png` (object style)

## Where it appears

The icon of the **Shield** power. It sits on a small button in the top bar during play, and on the locked reward card of the room that unlocks it. Unlocked by clearing the Mine. In play: For your next 4 turns, fire and ice can't hurt you: elemental traps, trap heads and fire or ice monsters deal no damage. It is shown small, so one bold shape beats detail.

## What it must look like

- The **Shield** power: a heraldic shield split down the middle, left half fire (`#f77622` / `#fee761`), right half ice (`#2ce8f5` / `#0099db`), with a gold rim (`#feae34`).
- It blocks fire and ice damage, so both halves must read.

## Do not

- Do not add a background or a frame: only the icon, on transparency.

## Deliver

`p_shield.png` (at least 256 × 256, transparent; see `art/README.md`). Optionally up to three variants as `p_shield-1.png`, `p_shield-2.png`, `p_shield-3.png`.
