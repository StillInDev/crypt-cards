How it plays
  • Swipe (or tap a neighbouring card, or use the arrow keys) to step onto an adjacent card. You use up that card. The cards behind you slide forward to fill the gap, and a new card drops in at the far edge of that row or column. Leave a corner and the cards along the edge behind it shift into the corner instead, with the new card appearing at the far end of that edge. Step into the middle square and the board turns clockwise instead: the card before your old square (going clockwise) slides into it, and the new card appears where that card was.
  • Monsters and traps cost you health equal to their number. Poison hurts 1 health a turn but never kills. A sword lets you attack a monster instead: if it dies, you take its place.
  • A chest costs a turn to open and leaves an item behind. Tapping your sword sells it for gold equal to its power. You can only hold one weapon, and picking up another replaces it, which also costs a turn.
  • Weapons come in fire and ice. A fire weapon cannot hurt fire monsters and hits ice monsters for double, and the other way round. An ice weapon freezes a monster it hits but does not kill: a frozen monster cannot be hit, so you swap places with it instead. Ninja stars hit only your row or column. With a bow in hand, tap any monster to shoot it; the shock jumps to monsters touching it. The scythe sweeps the target and the two squares beside it. Wands hit like a sword, but pierce: power left over after a monster carries on down the row or column into the monsters behind it. They follow the same fire and ice rules. Every monster also has a trick of its own; press and hold a card to read it. The Phoenix can only be worn down to 1 health. Step on it with no weapon, or hit it with an ice weapon, to kill it.
  • Press and hold any card, your hero, or a power to see what it does.
  • The chip above each neighbouring card shows what stepping there would do. A red skull means it would kill you.
  • Coins and gems are your score. Reach the room's target and a locked reward card (a symbol behind iron bars) drops onto the board. Step on it to unlock a hero, a power or the next rooms. A room opens once any room leading to it is cleared. Beat the boss at the bottom of an area for the key to the next one. Some side rooms are dead ends, but they always hold a hero or a power.
