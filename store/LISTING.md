**Full description (max 4000):**
Crypt Cards is a pixel-art dungeon puzzle where every swipe is a decision.

Your hero stands in a grid of cards. Swipe toward a card to step onto it: grab gold, drink potions, open chests, or fight monsters. The cards behind you slide forward and new ones appear at the edges, so the board never stops moving.

- Pick a hero and up to three powers. Unlock more by beating your own best score.
- Find weapons: swords, a scythe that cuts three cards at once, elemental wands, an electric bow whose shock chains between monsters, ninja stars, and jewel blades that grow stronger as you play.
- Fire and ice matter. Fire monsters shrug off flames, ice monsters laugh at frost, and each takes double from the other. Freeze a monster and you can't hit it, but you can swap places with it.
- Watch out for bombs, directional traps that turn every round, poison, shielded monsters in the deeper levels, and a web spider that only bites when your hands are empty.
- Climb a branching dungeon map. Each door opens at a score you set, and the map splits into paths with different requirements.
- Build your own levels with the built-in editor.

No ads, no accounts, no internet needed.
